# Insurance and Payments

## Accepted insurance plans

BrightSmile Dental works with these (fictional) dental plans:

- Evergreen Dental Plan
- Northwind Health PPO
- Cedar Shield Dental
- Harborlight Smile Care

We do not accept other plans at this time. If your plan is not listed, you can still be treated as a self-pay patient, and we can give you an itemized receipt to send to your insurer.

## How insurance works here

We submit claims directly to accepted plans. You pay your co-payment or the part not covered by your plan at the end of the visit. Coverage depends on your specific plan, so please bring your insurance card to the first visit and we will check your benefits.

## Payment methods

- Cash
- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard)
- Bank transfer

We do not accept personal checks.

## Payment plans

Treatments over $500 can be split into up to 6 monthly payments with no interest. A 20% deposit is required to start the plan. Ask the front desk to set it up before treatment begins.
