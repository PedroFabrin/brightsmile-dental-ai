# About BrightSmile Dental

BrightSmile Dental is a fictional family dental clinic created for this demo. All names, addresses, phone numbers and prices are made up.

## Our story

BrightSmile Dental opened in 2012 with a simple idea: dental care should be gentle, clear and free of surprises. What started as a two-chair office is now a modern clinic with five treatment rooms, a team of three dentists and two dental hygienists. We treat children from age 3, adults and seniors.

## Location
