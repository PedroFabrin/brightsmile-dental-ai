# Clinic Policies

## First visit

Please arrive 15 minutes early for your first visit. Bring a photo ID and your insurance card, if you have one. New patients fill out a short health form, which can also be completed online before the visit. The first visit takes about 60 minutes and includes an exam, X-rays and a treatment plan.

## Cancellations and rescheduling

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice are charged a $40 late cancellation fee.

## Missed appointments

A missed appointment without any notice (no-show) is charged $60. After two no-shows, we ask for a deposit to book new appointments.

## Late arrivals

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your appointment so we do not delay other patients. Please call us if you are running late.

## Children

Children under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. We treat children from age 3.

## Emergencies

Patients with dental emergencies during opening hours are seen the same day whenever possible. Call (555) 010-0143. For severe emergencies, go to the nearest hospital emergency room.

## Privacy

Your health information is used only for your care and is never shared without your permission. This assistant collects only the details needed to request an appointment.
