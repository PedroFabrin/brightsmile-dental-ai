# Opening Hours and Holidays

## Regular hours

- Monday to Thursday: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Friday: 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM
- Sunday: closed

All times are local clinic time. The last appointment of the day starts 30 minutes before closing.

## Holidays

The clinic is closed on these days:

- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Thanksgiving Day and the day after
- December 24 and December 25

On December 31 the clinic closes at 1:00 PM.

## Emergencies outside opening hours

The clinic does not have an after-hours line. For severe pain, heavy bleeding, facial swelling or trauma outside opening hours, go to the nearest hospital emergency room or call your local emergency number.
