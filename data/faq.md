# Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I book an appointment?

You can request an appointment through this chat assistant, by calling (555) 010-0142 or by emailing hello@brightsmile.example. A team member confirms every request.

### Where is the clinic located?

BrightSmile Dental is at 100 Example Avenue, Suite 4, Brightville, ST 00000 (fictional address). Free parking is available behind the building.

### What are your opening hours?

Monday to Thursday 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Friday 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays.

### Are you open on holidays?

No. We are closed on New Year's Day, July 4, Thanksgiving and the day after, and December 24 and 25.

### How much does a check-up cost?

A routine check-up and exam costs $75 for returning patients. A new patient exam with full-mouth X-rays costs $120.

### How much does teeth whitening cost?

In-office whitening costs $350 for one session, and the take-home kit costs $200.

### Which insurance plans do you accept?

We accept Evergreen Dental Plan, Northwind Health PPO, Cedar Shield Dental and Harborlight Smile Care.

### What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards, and bank transfer. We do not accept personal checks.

### Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Treatments over $500 can be split into up to 6 monthly payments with no interest, with a 20% deposit.

### What should I bring to my first visit?

A photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you take. Please arrive 15 minutes early.

### How long does the first visit take?

About 60 minutes, including the exam, X-rays and a treatment plan.

### What is your cancellation policy?

Please cancel at least 24 hours in advance. Late cancellations are charged $40, and no-shows are charged $60.

### What happens if I am late?

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your appointment.

### Do you treat children?

Yes, from age 3. A parent or guardian must stay with children under 18. A children's exam costs $65.

### Do you offer clear aligners?

Yes. Dr. Marcus Delgado leads clear aligner treatments, which cost between $3,800 and $5,500 depending on the case.

### Who performs root canals?

Dr. Priya Nandakumar, our endodontist. A root canal costs $700 to $1,100, depending on the tooth.

### What should I do in a dental emergency?

During opening hours, call our emergency line at (555) 010-0143 for a same-day visit. For severe pain, heavy bleeding, facial swelling or trauma, seek emergency medical care immediately. Outside opening hours, go to the nearest hospital emergency room.

### Which languages does the staff speak?

English and Portuguese.
