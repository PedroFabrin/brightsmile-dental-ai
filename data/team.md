# Our Team

All team members are fictional characters created for this demo.

## Dr. Amelia Hartwell, DDS

Founder and general dentist. Dr. Hartwell handles check-ups, fillings, crowns and preventive care for adults and children. She works Monday to Thursday.

## Dr. Marcus Delgado, DDS

Orthodontist. Dr. Delgado leads clear aligner treatments and bite correction for teenagers and adults. He works Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday.

## Dr. Priya Nandakumar, DDS

Endodontist and cosmetic dentist. Dr. Nandakumar performs root canals, teeth whitening and smile makeovers. She works Monday, Thursday and Friday.

## Hygiene team

Sam Okafor, RDH, and Lena Fischer, RDH, are our dental hygienists. They perform professional cleanings, fluoride treatments and oral hygiene coaching, Monday to Saturday during opening hours.

## Front desk

Our front desk team handles scheduling, insurance questions and payment plans during opening hours.
