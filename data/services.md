# Services and Prices

All prices are in US dollars (USD) and are estimates for patients without insurance. The final cost is confirmed by the dentist after the exam.

## Exams and prevention

- New patient exam with full-mouth X-rays: $120
- Routine check-up and exam (returning patients): $75
- Children's exam (ages 3 to 12): $65
- Professional teeth cleaning: $110
- Fluoride treatment: $30
- Dental X-rays (bitewing set): $60

## Restorative care

- Tooth-colored (composite) filling: $150 to $250, depending on size
- Simple tooth extraction: $180
- Root canal treatment: $700 to $1,100, depending on the tooth
- Porcelain crown: $1,100

## Cosmetic care

- In-office teeth whitening (one session): $350
- Take-home whitening kit: $200
- Clear aligners (full treatment): $3,800 to $5,500, depending on the case

## Other services

- Custom night guard: $400
- Emergency visit (pain, broken tooth, lost filling): $95 for the exam, treatment is quoted separately

## Payment plans

Treatments over $500 can be split into monthly payments. See the insurance and payments document for details.
